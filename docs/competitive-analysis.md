# VisionUp Competitive Analysis

## Goal

Compare existing solutions and identify where VisionUp can provide unique value for low vision users.

---

## macOS Accessibility

### Strengths

* Native integration
* Stable
* System-level zoom
* VoiceOver support
* High performance

### Weaknesses

* Limited customization
* Limited profile management
* No centralized accessibility workspace
* No dedicated productivity workflows for low vision users
* No secondary zoom display workflow

### Opportunity for VisionUp

* Accessibility profiles
* Advanced shortcut management
* Productivity-focused workflows
* Secondary zoom display
* Smart reading experience

---

## BetterDisplay

### Strengths

* Display scaling
* Virtual displays
* Advanced display controls

### Weaknesses

* Primarily display-focused
* Not designed specifically for low vision users
* Complex configuration
* Can introduce instability in some workflows

### Opportunity for VisionUp

* Low vision focused experience
* Simpler configuration
* Accessibility-first design
* Reading and navigation enhancements

---

## Zoom Accessibility Tools

### Strengths

* Magnification support
* Screen enlargement

### Weaknesses

* Single-purpose tools
* Limited integration
* Poor workflow support

### Opportunity for VisionUp

* Unified accessibility platform
* Profiles
* Reading mode
* Productivity features

---

# VisionUp Advantages

## Accessibility First

Designed specifically for low vision users.

## Productivity First

Focus on reducing effort and increasing efficiency.

## Unified Experience

Multiple accessibility functions managed from one application.

## Custom Profiles

Quick switching between accessibility configurations.

## Future Potential

* OCR
* AI Reading Assistant
* Smart Focus Tracking
* Advanced Multi-Display Accessibility

---

# VisionUp Positioning

VisionUp is not intended to replace macOS Accessibility.

VisionUp is not intended to replace BetterDisplay.

VisionUp extends existing accessibility capabilities by providing a unified, customizable, and productivity-focused experience for low vision users.
